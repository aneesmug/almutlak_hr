💡 HOW IT WORKS (SIMPLE EXAMPLE)

BEFORE (Without Holidays):
  Vacation: 15 days → Deduct: 15 days ❌

AFTER (With Holidays):
  Employee applies for vacation:    Jan 1-15 (15 days)
  System finds active holidays:     Jan 5-8 (Eid, 4 days)
  System calculates:                15 - 4 = 11 days
  System deducts:                   11 days from balance ✅
